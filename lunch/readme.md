# Turkey Pesto Sandwich

## Description

A toasted turkey sandwich with basil pesto, melted mozzarella, and fresh tomato on ciabatta. It's an Italian-deli style lunch that comes together in about 15 minutes, and the pesto adds a lot of flavor with very little work.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1 ciabatta roll (or 2 thick slices of sourdough)
- [ ] 2 tablespoons basil pesto
- [ ] 1 tablespoon mayonnaise (optional)
- [ ] 4 oz thinly sliced deli turkey
- [ ] 2 slices fresh mozzarella or provolone
- [ ] 2 to 3 slices tomato
- [ ] 1 small handful arugula or spinach
- [ ] 1 teaspoon olive oil or softened butter
- [ ] Pinch of salt and black pepper

### Steps for preparation:

1. Slice the ciabatta roll in half horizontally so you have a top and a bottom piece.
2. In a small bowl, stir the pesto and mayonnaise together until combined. If you're skipping the mayo, use the pesto on its own.
3. Spread the pesto mixture evenly over the cut side of both bread halves.
4. Layer the turkey on the bottom half, then lay the cheese slices on top of the turkey.
5. Close the sandwich and brush the outside of both halves lightly with olive oil or butter.
6. Heat a skillet or panini press over medium heat. Cook the sandwich 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing down gently with a spatula, until the bread is golden and the cheese has melted.
7. Open the sandwich, add the tomato slices, season them with salt and pepper, and top with the arugula.
8. Close the sandwich, cut it in half diagonally, and serve right away.

### Notes:

- Adding the tomato and greens after toasting keeps them fresh and stops them from wilting or making the bread soggy.
- Swap the mozzarella for provolone or Swiss if you want a sharper flavor.
- Add a few slices of roasted red pepper or sun-dried tomato for extra flavor.
- For a cold version, skip the oil and toasting steps and assemble everything at once.
- Store-bought pesto works fine. Jarred pesto is usually saltier than fresh, so taste before adding extra salt.
